## 3. PBR Material Architecture & Mesh Standards

Every generated model strictly adheres to the Khronos GLTF 2.0 Binary Specification:
1. `PaintMaterial` (ID 0): PBR Metallic Roughness (`baseColorFactor: [0.0, 0.898, 0.780, 1.0]`, `metallic: 0.85`, `roughness: 0.25`). Traversed by `Vehicle3DModal.tsx` for real-time dynamic body paint customization.
2. `DarkMatteMaterial` (ID 1): Low reflectance (`roughness: 0.85`, `metallic: 0.1`) for chassis tubes, seats, and cladding.
3. `ChromeMaterial` (ID 2): High specular metal (`metallic: 0.95`, `roughness: 0.1`) for engine fins, exhaust headers, and alloy rims.
4. `RubberMaterial` (ID 3): Deep carbon matte (`roughness: 0.9`) for tires.
5. `GlassMaterial` (ID 4): Alpha blended (`alphaMode: BLEND`, `transmission: 0.7`) for windshields and windows.
6. `HeadlightMaterial` (ID 5): Emissive white (`emissiveFactor: [0.95, 0.98, 1.0]`) toggled with the headlight spotlight.
7. `TaillightMaterial` (ID 6): Emissive red (`emissiveFactor: [0.85, 0.05, 0.05]`) for rear illumination.

## 4. Upgrade Path to Commercial 1:1 Digital Twins

For a full commercial release requiring exact per-model fidelity (e.g., exact Royal Enfield badging, exact Mahindra XUV700 AdrenoX dashboard geometry):
* **Licensing Requirement**: Purchase Commercial Extended licenses from 3D marketplaces (e.g. Sketchfab Editorial/Standard 3D License, TurboSquid Royalty Free License) or commission a 3D artist using Blender.
* **Format Specification**: Must export to standard `.glb` with embedded textures, $\le 50\text{k}$ triangles, and PBR standard materials named `PaintMaterial`.
* **Storage Location**: Place licensed files in `frontend/public/models/exact/{brand}_{model}.glb`.
* **Database Binding**: Enter the file path into the `model3d_url` column of the `vehicles` table. The frontend viewer prioritizes `vehicle.model3dUrl` before falling back to category silhouettes.
